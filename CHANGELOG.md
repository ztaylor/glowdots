# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- The engine: frames of any size with 2 to 16 shades and per-dot color, sprites (from dot maps, images, or pen-and-ink art), two pixel fonts, dithering and drawing helpers, and layout parts.
- The look of a 1990s plasma display on the GPU: round dots with white-hot centers, a halo, bloom, shimmer, and afterglow, all adjustable, with classic, LED, and worn presets. Colors and palettes per scene.
- A deck of scenes with a timer, taps and keys, and pinball transitions: wipe, dissolve, push, ink, and cut. Smaller scenes are centered on bigger panels.
- Feeds from a folder, from memory, or both, with errors the display can show.
- Players for a desktop window (macOS, Windows, Linux), a bare Raspberry Pi panel (SDL and KMSDRM), and a web page (WebGL 2), and offscreen PNG and GIF export.
- Example scenes: clock, weather, lake (and lake in winter), news board, events, sports, status board, and a demoscene tribute, with sample feeds.
- The showcase program, and the live web demo.
